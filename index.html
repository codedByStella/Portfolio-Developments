the pattern underneath all seven

Look across these seven projects and the tech stacks don't overlap much — Python, Node, PowerShell, ffmpeg, Discord's API, Socrata's API, Google's PageSpeed API. What does overlap is the design discipline: every one of them treats "the outside world will fail, lie, or go silent" as the default case, not the exception.

  • Verify, don't assume. The OSRS daemon won't trust a price without checking it against recent history first. StellaSnipes won't call a domain "clean" without an evidence trail behind that verdict. The red-team toolkit fails loud — [CRITICAL] — rather than giving a silent pass on an unconfirmed check.
  • Degrade gracefully, don't crash. Every external dependency across these projects — a price API, a domain-health service, a generative model — gets a timeout and a fallback path, so one flaky upstream service never takes the whole system down with it.
  • Keep a human in the loop where it actually matters. StellaStudios holds finished output for review before publishing. StellaSnipes routes unverifiable domains to a review queue instead of guessing. Automation that removes judgment entirely isn't more valuable — it's just wrong faster.

That's the actual pitch: not "I know five APIs," but "I build things that don't need me to babysit them, and when they can't be sure, they say so instead of guessing."
